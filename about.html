Our Story

Built by People Who Love Scent

NeoFragrances started with a simple frustration: authentic, well-made perfume was hard to find online without paying department-store markups or risking counterfeits. We set out to fix that — sourcing directly from licensed distributors and small independent houses, and pricing fairly.

To make authentic, high-quality fragrance accessible to everyone — no guesswork, no counterfeits, fair prices.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted fragrance destination in the region, known for authenticity and genuine care for customers.
